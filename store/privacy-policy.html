Trippi privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary

Trippi keeps your trips on your phone. We have no server and no accounts, and we never see the names, amounts or notes you enter.

Data stored on your phone

Trips, participant names, expenses, payments, categories and settings are stored only in the app on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use Back up (Premium), the backup file goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

Exchange rates

To show exchange rates, the app asks the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev, or api.frankfurter.app as a fallback) for reference rates published by the European Central Bank. These requests contain only currency codes and dates, never your trips or amounts. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to that service.

Advertising (free plan only)

On the free plan the app shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google your device's IP address (which gives an approximate location), advertising and app set identifiers, ad and app interactions (such as taps and video views), and diagnostic information (such as crash logs and performance). Google uses this to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. This data is encrypted in transit.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy options.
  • On iPhone, ads are always non-personalised and the app never asks to track you.
  • Premium subscribers see no ads, and no ad requests are made for them.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app never sees your payment details. It only asks the store whether your subscription is active.

Children

Trippi is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

You can delete any trip in the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to delete or export on request. For data Google holds for advertising, see Google's privacy policy above.
