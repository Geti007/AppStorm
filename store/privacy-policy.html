Glassful privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Glassful is a water tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Glassful app on Android or iPhone.

What Glassful stores

Glassful stores the drinks you log (the time and the amount), your daily goal and the history of goal changes, your choice of millilitres or fluid ounces, and whether you have finished the welcome screen. This information is kept only on your device, in the app's private storage. Glassful has no account, no sign-in and no server, and it never sends this information to us or to anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glassful is free and shows one banner ad on the History screen. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent

When the law where you live requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Glassful asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, under "Privacy options", when that option applies to you. On iPhone, Glassful requests only non-personalised ads and does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Children

Glassful is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (maximum rating PG).

Deleting your data

You can delete everything Glassful stores at any time with Settings, then "Reset all data". Uninstalling the app also removes all of it from your device. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.
