# App idea (from the owner, 2026-10-11)

Trippi – Group Travel Expense Tracker

Build a modern, intuitive mobile app for iOS and Android that helps friends, couples, and groups easily manage and split expenses while traveling. Users can create trips, invite friends, record shared expenses, and automatically calculate who owes whom.

## Core features

- Create trips and invite participants.
- Add expenses for hotels, restaurants, transportation, activities, and more.
- Split expenses equally, by percentage, or by custom amounts.
- Automatically calculate balances and simplify debts.
- Support multiple currencies with exchange rate conversion.
- Track spending with a visual dashboard.
- Mark debts as settled and maintain payment history.
- Share expense summaries.
- Offline expense tracking with automatic syncing.
- Beautiful, modern UI with light and dark modes.

## Monetization: free with ads + Premium subscription

Free version (ad-supported): a smart, non-intrusive advertising system.
- Small native ads that blend into the trip dashboard or expense history.
- Occasional rewarded video ads users can choose to watch to unlock temporary premium features.
- Never interrupt users while adding expenses, splitting bills, or settling payments.
- No aggressive pop-ups or frequent full-screen ads.
- Limit ad frequency; avoid repeatedly showing the same ad.
- Clearly label ads as sponsored content.

Trippi Premium: $1.99/month with a 7-day free trial. Premium gets:
- Completely ad-free experience.
- Unlimited trips and participants.
- Advanced spending analytics and detailed charts.
- PDF and Excel expense exports.
- Receipt scanning with AI-powered expense recognition.
- Advanced currency conversion and historical exchange rates.
- Custom expense categories.
- Cloud backup and cross-device synchronization.
- Premium app themes and customization.

The free version should remain genuinely useful; Premium adds convenience and advanced capabilities.

Subscription experience:
- 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers; then renews at $1.99/month unless canceled.
- Show the monthly price clearly before subscribing; explain when the trial ends and billing begins.
- Easy manage/cancel.
- Optional, elegant upgrade prompts at natural moments, never interrupting important actions.
- Apple App Store and Google Play subscription billing.

## Design

Premium-looking interface: smooth animations, rounded cards, beautiful charts, travel-inspired visuals, intuitive navigation.

- Brand name: Trippi
- App subtitle: Group Travel Expense Tracker
- Tagline: Travel Together. Split Smarter.

Trippi should feel like a polished financial travel companion rather than an advertising platform. Prioritize retention, simplicity, performance, and sustainable monetization.
